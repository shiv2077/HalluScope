# itemtriage Phase 1 findings

Generated by `python3 -m itemtriage.cli findings` from `data/itemtriage/`. Proportions carry Wilson intervals.

## Data

MMLU-Redux 2.0 (`edinburgh-dawg/mmlu-redux-2.0`, CC-BY-4.0): 5700 items, 57 subjects of 100, 4 options each. Columns: question, choices, answer, error_type, source, correct_answer, potential_reason. Only question, choices and answer enter `items.parquet`. The rest go to `labels.parquet`.

| error_type | items | class |
|---|---:|---|
| `ok` | 5330 | negative |
| `bad_question_clarity` | 132 | secondary only |
| `wrong_groundtruth` | 106 | primary and secondary |
| `multiple_correct_answers` | 39 | primary and secondary |
| `no_correct_answer` | 36 | primary and secondary |
| `expert` | 32 | unknown, excluded |
| `bad_options_clarity` | 25 | secondary only |

`expert` marks items the annotator could not verify, so it is unknown and excluded from both classes. Identity is question plus options. 64 items share an identical question and options with another item, all within the same fold.

Writer `IRT-mislabeled-items` (ODC-BY) holds 1,001 flagged items only, not every benchmark item. Its multiple-choice weak positives are 12 for GSM-MC and 9 for MATH-MC, and GPQA is treated as all correct. No external benchmark reaches 100 weak positives, so H1b is not computable and no external benchmark is run. The item-level panel responses (`evaleval/EEE_datastore`) are not public under that name. A public copy holds GSM-MC and MATH-MC responses, which is moot given the label count.

## Panel

7 models in 5 families. Local models fit in 6 GB: fp16 at 1.7B or less, NF4 above. OLMo-2-1B (AI2) is the reserve. Selection is confirmed on a 300-item calibration pilot: letter parse rate at least 95%, and accuracy above chance by its Wilson lower bound.
